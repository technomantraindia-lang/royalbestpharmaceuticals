LABORATORY CAPABILITIES

Advanced Analytical Testing Equipment

Our Quality Control laboratory in Vapi GIDC is equipped with high-precision analytical instruments to conduct comprehensive chemical, physical, and microbiological assays.

HPLC & UPLC Chromatography

High-Performance and Ultra-Performance Liquid Chromatography with Photodiode Array (PDA) detectors for assay quantification, enantiomeric purity, and related substances detection.

Assay & Purity Quantification

Gas Chromatography (GC-MS / FID)

Headspace Gas Chromatography for high-sensitivity quantification of Class 1, 2, and 3 residual organic solvents adhering strictly to ICH Q3C compendial guidelines.

ICH Q3C Residual Solvents

FTIR & UV-Vis Spectroscopy

Fourier Transform Infrared and UV-Visible spectrophotometers for rapid molecular identity confirmation, absorption profiles, and spectral fingerprinting against pharmacopoeial references.

Spectral Fingerprinting

Karl Fischer & Moisture Titration

Coulometric and volumetric Karl Fischer titrators alongside Loss on Drying (LOD) micro-balances for accurate water determination in hygroscopic and sterile active substances.

Accurate Moisture Limits

ICP-MS & Elemental Impurities

Inductively Coupled Plasma Mass Spectrometry for high-sensitivity screening and quantitative determination of Class 1, 2, and 3 toxic heavy metals adhering strictly to ICH Q3D compendia.

ICH Q3D Heavy Metal Limits

Microbiology & Bioburden Suite

Dedicated sterile testing suites with laminar airflow hoods, calibrated incubators, and bio-burden filtration units for total microbial count, pathogen absence, and endotoxin LAL testing.

Sterility & Endotoxin Limits
