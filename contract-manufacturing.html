Tablets (Oral Solid Dosage)

High-speed rotary tablet presses with precision weight control, automatic hardness testing, and automated film/enteric coating pans.

  • Uncoated, Film-Coated & Enteric-Coated Tablets
  • Bilayer & Sustained Release (SR / PR) Tablets
  • Chewable, Dispersible & Effervescent Tablets
  • Packaging: Alu-Alu, Blister (PVC/PVDC), Strip & HDPE Bottles

Hard Gelatin & HPMC Capsules

Automatic capsule filling machines equipped with tamping pin dosing and pellet filling attachments for multi-particulate systems.

  • Powder Filled & Pellet Filled Capsules (Sizes 00 to 4)
  • Dual-Release (Tablet in Capsule / Pellet in Capsule)
  • 100% Vegetarian HPMC & Gelatin Shells
  • Packaging: Alu-Alu, Blister, Container Packing

Sterile Injectables & Lyophilized Vials

Class 100 / Grade A aseptic processing suites with nitrogen purging, sterile tunnel depyrogenation, and continuous particulate monitoring.

  • Small Volume Parenterals (SVP Ampoules 1ml - 20ml)
  • Lyophilized (Freeze-Dried) Sterile Vials
  • Dry Powder Beta-Lactam / Cephalosporin Injectable Vials
  • Endotoxin Testing & 100% Visual / Optical Inspection

Topical Ointments, Creams & Gels

High-shear planetary mixing and vacuum homogenizers for smooth, ultra-stable dermatological and steroid emulsions.

  • Corticosteroid Creams & Ointments (Betamethasone, Clobetasol)
  • Antifungal & Antibacterial Gels / Lotions
  • Automated Lami-tube and Aluminum tube sealing
  • Pack Sizes: 5g, 10g, 15g, 20g, 30g, 50g tubes

Liquid Orals, Syrups & Suspensions

Automated stainless steel manufacturing vessels (SS 316L) with multi-stage filtration and high-speed volumetric bottle filling lines.

  • Cough Syrups, Antihistaminic & Bronchodilator Liquids
  • Pediatric Suspensions & Multi-vitamin Drops
  • Amber PET & Glass Bottles with Measuring Caps
  • Pack Sizes: 30ml, 60ml, 100ml, 200ml, 450ml

Our 5-Stage Contract Manufacturing Workflow

From initial formula evaluation to commercial batch dispatch, our structured project management ensures on-time delivery and uncompromising quality.

01

Inquiry & Feasibility

Share your target formulation, active ingredients, dosage form, batch size, and packaging requirements for cost and technical feasibility assessment.

02

Formulation & Pilot Trials

R&D lab batch formulation, excipient compatibility testing, dissolution profiling, and analytical method optimization.

03

Licensing & Approvals

State Licensing Authority (SLA) & DCGI product permission filing, brand name registration, and packaging artwork approvals.

04

Commercial Production

Full-scale manufacturing in our Baddi or Maharashtra facilities under stringent in-process quality controls and cleanroom protocols.

05

QC Release & Logistics

Complete batch Certificate of Analysis (COA) generation, QA sign-off, and temperature-controlled dispatch to domestic or export ports.
