STERILITY ASSURANCE

Strict Parenteral Quality Controls

Our sterile bulk pharmaceutical ingredients are produced in Class 100 (ISO Class 5) aseptic crystallization suites. Every batch undergoes mandatory 14-day membrane filtration sterility testing according to USP <71> and EP 2.6.1.

Bacterial Endotoxins (BET) testing via kinetic chromogenic LAL ensures levels strictly below 0.05 EU/mg, suitable for direct sterile reconstitution and vial filling.
